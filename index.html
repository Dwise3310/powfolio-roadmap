Adapted from Bug Bounty Playbook + Crypto Twitter 2026 · 16 Oct 2026

Closed beta, X warmup, then launch.

Solo founder, pharmacy student, first project. Private first. Do not open a public bounty. Do not treat X as a broadcast channel. Sequence protects study time.

Why private, not public

From the Bug Bounty Playbook

Start with a private program. Invite specific people (10–20). Controlled volume, higher signal, fewer invalid reports. You can fix the rules while the group is small. Most programs stay private for 3–12 months. Launching public on day one is the common mistake: 200 reports, 180 noise, triage burnout, valid bugs sit in a queue.

Prerequisites before any paid program: low-hanging fruit already fixed, engineering capacity to respond, clear scope, and a budget you can actually pay. A ₦50k pool fails all of those for a public audience. Call it early access, not a bounty.

1. Private closed beta

School Telegram · 7–10 days · ₦50k hard cap

  1. Invite ~25 people you already know. Pin the rules before anyone starts testing.
  2. Every report needs: steps to reproduce, device + browser, and a screenshot or short video. “It broke” is rejected.
  3. Severity: Critical (auth bypass or data leak) ₦5,000 · High (core flow broken for many) ₦3–4,000 · Medium (feature fails on mobile or data not saving) ₦2,000 · Low/UI: shoutout or nothing.
  4. First valid reports only. Total pool ₦50k. Close on a fixed date. Acknowledge within 2 days. Pay only after you confirm the bug yourself.
  5. Out of scope: social engineering, DoS, spam, reports with no steps, issues already fixed, feature requests.
  6. Fix critical bugs in batches (one sitting), not one-by-one all day. Medium and low can wait until after launch if time is tight.
  7. Later, if you want a formal channel: add /.well-known/security.txt and a short VDP page. Not required for this round.
2. X presence — Crypto Twitter 2026

Blue check + 2–3 weeks of posts before the launch post

CT trusts people over logos. Accounts that appear 2–4 weeks before launch read as farmed. Organic presence takes 6–12 months; KOL-accelerated can be 6–10 weeks. You are doing the minimum credible window.

  1. Get the blue check this week. Premium on web (~$8/mo). Basic does not include the check. Buy on web, not the app (app has extra fees).
  2. Post 4–5 times a week from the founder account (@cryptoadvancers). Not the brand handle alone.
  3. Content mix (CT Signal Ladder): Builder Signal (progress, scores, proof sections, screenshots) + Narrative (why scattered Web3 work is broken) + Community (replies, early profiles). Authority (KOL mentions) comes later.
  4. Reply to 8–10 relevant posts a day (trading, airdrops, community leads, researchers). No “great project” spam. Genuine takes only.
  5. High-performing formats to use: mechanism breakdown (how the two scores work), contrarian take (most profiles are claims), build-in-public update (what you fixed this week).
  6. First posts are build-in-public. The launch announcement comes after the private beta closes and critical bugs are fixed.
  7. X Score (influential followers) matters more than raw count for later partnerships. Grow it by being useful in threads, not by buying followers.
  8. Optional later: one or two micro-KOL mentions once you have 20 real profiles. Do not buy engagement or fake followers.
3. Solo student sequence

Protect study time. Batch everything.

  1. This week: Buy domain (powfolio.xyz, WhoisGuard, no hosting). Get blue check. Pin the beta rules. Finish landing page polish. Write the first 5 X posts in one sitting.
  2. Days 1–10: Private Telegram group only. Collect reports. Fix critical bugs in one or two batches. Do not context-switch every hour.
  3. Parallel: 4–5 X posts a week + daily replies. Schedule posts so school days stay clear.
  4. Launch week: One clear launch post. Share the link. Ask the 25 to post their profiles. Watch the ops inbox. Then go quiet for a full day.
  5. After launch: Weekly rhythm only — one profile note, one product fix, one community prompt. No daily firefighting. Directory only after 20 finished public profiles exist.
What we are not doing

Deliberate skips

  • No public bug bounty. Reward is too small and triage will bury a solo founder.
  • No paid ads or follower buys before the first 20 real profiles.
  • No token talk. This is a portfolio, not a TGE.
  • No directory until 20 finished public profiles exist.
  • No daily posting once school resumes. Batch and schedule.
