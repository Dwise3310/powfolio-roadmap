Namecheap, then Vercel.

  1. Account at namecheap.com with an email you still control. Save the login outside the browser.
  2. Search powfolio.xyz first. If it is a premium listing, compare pow3folio.xyz. Do not buy hosting.
  3. Enable WhoisGuard. It is free and hides the personal address from public whois.
  4. Pay, turn on auto-renew, and write down the renewal date.
  5. Domain List → Manage → Advanced DNS. Delete the parking URL redirect.
  6. A record: host @, value 76.76.21.21. CNAME: host www, value cname.vercel-dns.com.
  7. Vercel project pow3folio → Settings → Domains. Add the apex and www. Wait until both say valid and the certificate is issued.
  8. Set the apex as primary. Keep vercel.app as a redirect.
  9. Change the privacy contact from privacy@pow3folio.vercel.app to an address on the new domain.
  10. Do not switch nameservers unless you want Vercel to own every DNS record. A and CNAME are enough.

Closed beta, X warmup, then launch.

Solo founder, pharmacy student, first project. Private first. Do not open a public bounty. Do not treat X as a broadcast channel.

1. Private closed beta

School Telegram · 7–10 days · ₦50k cap

Invite ~25 people. Pin rules. Require steps + screenshot. Critical ₦5k · High ₦3–4k · Medium ₦2k · Low: shoutout. First valid only. Close on a fixed date. Acknowledge in 2 days. Pay after you confirm.

2. X presence

Blue check + 2–3 weeks of posts

Premium on web (~$8/mo). Post 4–5 times a week from the founder account. Reply to 8–10 relevant posts a day. Mix builder signal, narrative, and community. Launch announcement comes after the private beta closes.

3. Solo sequence

Protect study time

This week: domain, blue check, pin rules, write first posts. Days 1–10: private group, fix critical bugs in batches. Parallel: scheduled posts. Launch week: one clear post, ask the 25 to share profiles, then quiet for a day. After: weekly rhythm only.

What we skip

Deliberate

No public bounty. No paid ads before 20 real profiles. No token talk. No directory until those profiles exist. No daily posting once school resumes.

After the domain is live

Email with Resend.

  1. Buy powfolio.xyz first. Resend needs a verified domain to send from.
  2. Sign up at resend.com with the Powfolio Gmail (Powfolio.xyz@gmail.com is fine as the account login).
  3. Add the domain powfolio.xyz in Resend. Copy the DNS records they give you (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  4. In Namecheap Advanced DNS, add those records. Wait until Resend shows verified.
  5. Create an API key in Resend. Copy it once.
  6. In Vercel → pow3folio → Settings → Environment Variables, add:
    RESEND_API_KEY = the key
    MAIL_FROM = Powfolio
  7. Redeploy. Test signup OTP. You should receive the code from noreply@powfolio.xyz.
  8. The Gmail is only for the Resend account login. Outgoing mail uses the domain, not Gmail.

Templates already exist for OTP, welcome, suspension, appeal, and password reset. Once the two env vars are set, OTP and suspension send. The rest can be wired after the domain is live.
