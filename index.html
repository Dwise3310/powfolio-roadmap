Namecheap to Vercel

Buy and point the domain.

  1. Create or sign in at namecheap.com. Use an email you will still have in two years.
  2. Search powfolio.xyz first, then pow3folio.xyz. Prefer .xyz if the price is sane. Avoid premium listings above your budget.
  3. Add the domain only. Decline hosting, email bundles, and SSL add-ons. Vercel issues the certificate.
  4. Turn on WhoisGuard, Namecheap's free privacy. It hides the personal address from the public whois record.
  5. Pay with a card in your name. Save the receipt and the renewal date. Turn on auto-renew.
  6. Open Domain List, then Manage, then Advanced DNS. Delete parking records that Namecheap adds.
  7. Add an A record: host @, value 76.76.21.21. Add a CNAME: host www, value cname.vercel-dns.com.
  8. In the Powfolio Vercel project, Settings, Domains, add powfolio.xyz and www.powfolio.xyz. Wait until both say valid.
  9. Set the apex as primary. Keep the vercel.app address as a redirect so old links still work.
  10. Replace privacy@pow3folio.vercel.app in the privacy page with an address on the new domain.

Tip: do not change nameservers to Vercel unless you want Vercel to host all DNS. The A plus CNAME path above is enough and easier to undo.

Nigeria first, then public

Legal steps.

  1. Search the Powfolio name on the Nigerian trademark portal and a basic web search before spending on a mark.
  2. If you will take money, register the business with CAC. A business name is enough to start. A limited company can wait.
  3. Rewrite privacy and terms onto the live domain. Name the controller, the contact email, and what Supabase, Vercel, and Google store.
  4. NDPR: say why you collect email, wallet address, and location. Location should stay optional. Keep a deletion path, which already exists.
  5. Cookie banner already exists. Confirm it blocks non-essential tags until accepted.
  6. Add a short content rule: users own their proof, Powfolio can remove reported abuse. The report and appeal desks already exist in ops.
  7. Do not claim scores are verified income. Call them profile and builder scores.
After the link is stable

Social, marketing, and scale.

  1. Publish your own profile as the first proof. The public page has to survive a cold visit.
  2. One X thread from @cryptoadvancers: what Powfolio is, one screenshot, the link. No paid promo yet.
  3. Seed 20 real profiles from Cryptoadvancers before a directory exists. Empty discovery looks broken.
  4. Weekly: one public profile teardown, one product note, one community prompt.
  5. After 50 public profiles, build the directory. Filter by skill, chain, and open-to-work.
  6. Employer path comes after that: a shortlist and a contact, not a job board.
  7. Scale later means backups, status page, and a second admin. Not a second product.
