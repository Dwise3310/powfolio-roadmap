Namecheap, then Vercel.

  1. Account at namecheap.com with an email you still control. Save the login outside the browser.
  2. Search powfolio.xyz first. If it is a premium listing, compare pow3folio.xyz. Do not buy hosting.
  3. Enable WhoisGuard. It is free and hides the personal address from public whois.
  4. Pay, turn on auto-renew, and write down the renewal date.
  5. Domain List, Manage, Advanced DNS. Delete the parking URL redirect.
  6. A record: host @, value 76.76.21.21. CNAME: host www, value cname.vercel-dns.com.
  7. Vercel project pow3folio, Settings, Domains. Add the apex and www. Wait until both say valid and the certificate is issued.
  8. Set the apex as primary. Keep vercel.app as a redirect.
  9. Change the privacy contact from privacy@pow3folio.vercel.app to an address on the new domain.
  10. Do not switch nameservers unless you want Vercel to own every DNS record. A and CNAME are enough.

Adapted from Bug Bounty Playbook + Crypto Twitter 2026

Closed beta, X warmup, then launch.

Solo founder, pharmacy student, first project. Private first. Do not open a public bounty. Do not treat X as a broadcast channel.

1. Private closed beta (not a public bounty)

School Telegram group · 7–10 days · ₦50k cap

Start private. Control volume and signal. Public on day one drowns a solo founder in noise. Most programs stay private for months. Call it early access, not a formal bug bounty.

  1. Invite ~25 people you already know. Pin rules before anyone starts.
  2. Require steps to reproduce, device/browser, and a screenshot or short video. “It broke” is not a report.
  3. Tiers: Critical (auth/data leak) ₦5,000 · High (core flow broken) ₦3–4,000 · Medium (feature fails on mobile) ₦2,000 · Low/UI: shoutout or nothing.
  4. First valid reports only. Close on a fixed date. Acknowledge within 2 days. Pay only after you confirm.
  5. Out of scope: social engineering, DoS, spam, reports with no steps, issues already fixed.
  6. Add a simple security.txt and a short policy note later if you want a VDP. For now the Telegram pin is enough.
2. X presence (Crypto Twitter 2026)

Blue check + 2–3 weeks of posts before the launch post

Accounts that appear 2–4 weeks before launch read as farmed. CT trusts people over logos. Mix builder signal with real replies. X Score (influential followers) matters more than raw count for later partnerships.

  1. Get the blue check active this week (Premium on web, ~$8/mo). Basic does not include it.
  2. Post 4–5 times a week from the founder account. One clear problem, one screenshot, one short build update, one reply-style take.
  3. Reply to 8–10 relevant posts a day (trading, airdrops, community leads). No “great project” spam.
  4. Content mix: Builder Signal (progress, scores, proof sections) + Narrative (why scattered Web3 work is broken) + Community (replies and early profiles).
  5. First posts are build-in-public, not a launch announcement. Launch post comes after the private beta closes.
  6. Optional later: one or two micro-KOL mentions once you have 20 real profiles. Do not buy engagement.
3. Solo student sequence

Protect study time. Batch everything.

  1. This week: domain, blue check, pin the beta rules, finish landing polish.
  2. Next 7–10 days: private group only. Fix critical bugs in batches, not one by one all day.
  3. Parallel: 4–5 X posts a week. Write them in one sitting and schedule.
  4. Launch week: one clear post, share the link, ask the 25 to post their profiles, then go quiet for a day.
  5. After launch: weekly rhythm only — one profile note, one product fix, one community prompt. No daily firefighting.
What we are not doing

Deliberate skips

  • No public bug bounty. Reward is too small and triage will bury you.
  • No paid ads or follower buys before the first 20 real profiles.
  • No token talk. This is a portfolio, not a TGE.
  • No directory until 20 finished public profiles exist.
